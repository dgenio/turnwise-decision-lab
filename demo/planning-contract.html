The result that matters
The existing eight-case smoke benchmark does not show decision-model advantage: a simple rule policy matches all eight authored labels without a classifier. This exhibit shows implementation contracts, not real-world accuracy. To establish additional value we would need independent multi-turn tasks, verified outcomes, a provider-native comparator, safety checks, and real latency/cost data.
